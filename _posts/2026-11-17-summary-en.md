### [Unit 42: HTTP/3-Powered RATs Target Southeast Asian Governments](https://unit42.paloaltonetworks.com/custom-rats-target-southeast-asia-governments/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Unit 42 researchers reported a cyberespionage campaign targeting government entities in Southeast Asia with two custom remote access trojans, PerfectPioneer and KUKURAT, that use HTTP/3 and QUIC protocols for command-and-control communications. The campaign leverages the newer HTTP/3 standard to evade traditional network monitoring. Palo Alto Networks&\#x27; Unit 42 identified the activity but did not attribute it to a specific threat actor in the initial publication.

rss · Unit 42 · Nov 16, 23:00

**「Background」** HTTP/3, which runs over QUIC, is increasingly adopted for legitimate web traffic, making it an attractive channel for covert communications. Previous Unit 42 research has documented Southeast Asian governments as frequent targets of state-aligned espionage operations.

**「Impact」** The adoption of HTTP/3 for C2 requires defenders to update traffic inspection capabilities and behavioral analytics to detect malicious use of the protocol.

### [GAO Warns Space Industrial Base Strained by $33B LEO Constellation Shift](https://defensescoop.com/2026/11/17/space-industrial-base-stretched-thin-gao-report/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The Government Accountability Office reported Tuesday that the Pentagon&\#x27;s $33 billion shift to massed low-Earth orbit satellite constellations over the next five years is stressing the specialized space industrial base, threatening Space Force procurement. GAO found intense competition for semiconductors, electronics, raw materials, and manufacturing capacity, with 94 percent of U.S. satellites now privately owned. The watchdog recommended centralizing supply chain oversight across the Department of the Air Force and National Reconnaissance Office, and establishing clear leadership for enterprise risk management. The Defense Department and Air Force agreed with all three recommendations.

rss · DefenseScoop · Nov 17, 20:15

**「Background」** In 2020 the Defense Department launched the Proliferated Warfighter Space Architecture to replace reliance on large geostationary satellites with resilient constellations of small LEO satellites. GAO assessed three programs — PWSA, GPS IIIF, and MWT MEO — from August 2025 to November 2026, finding PWSA already facing missed deadlines and delivery delays from supply chain disruptions.

**「Impact」** Supply chain bottlenecks including printed circuit board shortages have already delayed launches for the $8.3 billion MWT MEO missile warning program and the $15.6 billion PWSA, with program officials reporting in April 2026 that parts availability continues to push back planned launches.

**Tags**: `#GEOINT`, `#Space Force`, `#industrial base`, `#GAO`, `#satellite constellations`

### [Pakistani hackers target defense workers via fake job sites](https://cyberscoop.com/pakistani-hackers-defense-workers-fake-job-sites/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

CyberScoop reported that Lookout researchers uncovered an ongoing Pakistani-aligned cyberespionage campaign using fake job-recruiter websites to deliver GravityRAT spyware to Indian defense contractors and military personnel. The activity, attributed to the group known as SpaceCobra or Cosmic Leopard, has been observed over the past 12 months and remains active. Lookout found the attackers built realistic sites featuring logos of major defense firms such as Airbus, BAE Systems, Lockheed Martin, and Northrop Grumman, and in at least one case the site made no reference to India, suggesting broader targeting of defense personnel worldwide. The malware, active since 2015, steals contacts, call logs, texts, and WhatsApp messages.

rss · CyberScoop · Nov 17, 14:00

**「Background」** GravityRAT has been associated with Pakistani-aligned threat actors since at least 2015 and is typically used for data theft from mobile devices. SpaceCobra, also tracked as Cosmic Leopard, has a history of targeting Indian defense and government entities through social-engineering lures.

**「Impact」** Defense-sector organizations and their personnel globally face an elevated risk of credential theft and device compromise from convincing recruiter-themed lures that bypass traditional phishing indicators, requiring updated mobile threat defenses and awareness training.

**Tags**: `#cyber threat intelligence`, `#APT`, `#GravityRAT`, `#social engineering`, `#defense sector`
