### [BIS Issues Interim Final Rule Restricting AI Chip Exports to Embargoed Nations](https://cyberscoop.com/commerce-ai-chip-export-restrictions/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The Bureau of Industry and Security issued a 132-page interim final rule on Monday restricting U.S. exports of advanced AI chips to third-party data centers in more than 20 arms-embargoed countries, including China, Iran, and North Korea. The rule also imposes new licensing requirements for chip sales to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Brazil, which Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick determined pose a &quot;higher-than-normal risk&quot; of diversion. Companies with pending or ongoing sales have 90 days to obtain the new licenses, which contain fewer exemptions for overseas chip designers. BIS cited authority under the 2018 Export Control Reform Act to bypass normal notice-and-comment rulemaking.

rss · CyberScoop · Nov 16, 21:13

**「Background」** The rule aims to close loopholes that allowed companies headquartered in countries of concern to access U.S. advanced semiconductors for training AI models through third-party data center arrangements. U.S. lawmakers have long cited semiconductor dominance as a strategic advantage over rivals like China, which is developing its domestic AI chip industry.

**「Impact」** The restrictions will force U.S. chipmakers and foreign data centers to implement enhanced due diligence on customer ownership and control, potentially disrupting existing supply chains and delaying AI infrastructure deployments in the newly licensed countries.
