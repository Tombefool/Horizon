# Global intelligence industry profile

Use this profile for timely public reporting on the intelligence industry and related disciplines, including:

- Intelligence agencies, oversight bodies, and policy or legal changes that affect intelligence authorities, budgets, leadership, or accountability.
- Open-source intelligence (OSINT): methods, tools, investigations, and professional practice.
- Cyber threat intelligence: actor reporting, campaigns, official advisories and joint alerts, and significant research from threat-intelligence teams.
- Commercial intelligence, investigations, and threat-intelligence companies, including products, contracts, mergers, funding, and leadership changes.
- Geospatial and satellite intelligence (GEOINT), plus technology, imagery, or companies that materially serve intelligence or reconnaissance missions.

Typical items include official announcements, reputable news reports, substantial research publications, and industry developments with a clear intelligence-community or intelligence-industry hook.

Do not use this profile for generic software or AI developer news, routine CVE or patch-Tuesday roundups, consumer cybersecurity tips, stock-price commentary, marketing posts without substantive information, ordinary crime reporting, or geopolitical or military coverage that is not specifically about intelligence agencies, OSINT, threat intelligence, commercial intelligence firms, or geospatial/satellite intelligence.
